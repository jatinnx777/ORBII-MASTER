The situation

The problem isn't that she
has no safety app.

It's that every one needs her to reach it. Open it, find the button, press, hold three seconds. But in the moment that matters — grabbed, running, phone buried in a bag, hands not free — the app is built for the exact second she can't use it. Panic comes first. Help comes only after she can act. Women get hurt in the gap between the two.

Why today's apps fail

Two broken promises.

They need the button

Every panic-button app assumes a free hand and a calm she doesn't have. The one design they're built around is the one she can't perform in an emergency.

They broke the trust

The world's biggest family-safety app was caught selling users' precise location — including children's — to data brokers. "Kept safe" quietly became "watched and sold."

Our insight

So we got rid
of the button.

ORBII doesn't wait for her to act. It listens.

The product · start to finish

She says one word.
She never touches the screen.

01 · SHE SPEAKS

"Help."

ORBII hears it — screen off, phone in a pocket, on a noisy street. We tuned the listening for exactly those conditions.

02 · SAFETY GATE

10-second countdown

One tap cancels a false alarm, so nobody ever gets a fake SOS. Accidental triggers can't get through.

03 · HELP FIRES

Three things at once

Her circle gets her live location, every nearby ORBII member is alerted, and evidence recording begins — hands-free.

Why she can trust it

On her phone. On her terms.
Never for sale.

The audio never leaves the device

Listening happens entirely on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, so there's nothing to leak and nothing to sell. Privacy isn't a policy here — it's the architecture.

She always sees who can see her

ORBII shows exactly who can view her location and lets her change it in one tap. Life360 hides the watching. We put it on the screen.

That's not a feature. It's the whole difference between safety and surveillance — and it's the market Life360 left wide open.

Who actually comes

A safety baseline for everyone.
Guaranteed response for members.

Free · the baseline

Her circle is alerted, nearby ORBII volunteers get a full-screen call for help, and she can dial 112 in one tap. Never behind a paywall.

Plus · verified responders

Background-checked, rated, and paid per response — dispatched and accountable. Their job is presence, not combat: reach her, be a witness, make noise, get police there.

Why now

The most important safety
data in India doesn't exist yet.

Most crimes against women are never reported, so official statistics are blind to where and when women actually feel unsafe. ORBII generates that data in real time — every SOS, every avoided street — which is exactly why it becomes indispensable to families first, and to city governments next.
