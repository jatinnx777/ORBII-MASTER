What ORBII does

ORBII is a personal-safety app. When you trigger an SOS, the app shares your live location, name, and phone number, and a brief incident record, with the people you have chosen and with nearby ORBII users, so help can reach you as quickly as possible. Everything ORBII does is in service of a safety feature you explicitly turn on or trigger.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile number, used for sign-in and so responders can call you.
  • Your name and an optional profile photo.
  • Your emergency-contact list (each contact's name and phone number).
  • Your precise location only while an SOS or a safety journey is active.
  • A timestamped record of each SOS you trigger, kept for your own history.

Voice detection stays on your device

ORBII's Voice SOS listens for a distress word entirely on your phone. The audio is processed on the device to recognise the word and is never uploaded, streamed, or stored on any server. There is nothing for us to sell or leak, because your audio never leaves your phone.

In short: the microphone is used only to detect your trigger word, on your device, and no recording is sent to us.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to deliver the safety features you trigger: raising an SOS, sharing your live location with your circle, alerting nearby responders, and showing you your own incident history. We do not use your data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

Who can see your data

  • Your circle and emergency contacts see the information you choose to share with them, such as your live location during an SOS or safety journey.
  • Nearby responders who receive your SOS see your name, approximate location, and phone number, for that incident only.
  • Our team may review aggregated, de-identified incident logs to keep the service safe and working. We do not browse individual users' data.

We never sell your data

ORBII does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers, advertisers, or anyone else, under any circumstances. This is a permanent commitment, not a current policy that could quietly change.

Your rights

You are in control of your data. You can:

  • Delete your account at any time from Profile → Settings. Deletion removes your profile, contacts, and history.
  • Access or export the information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent for any data use by deleting your account.

To make any of these requests, or to raise a concern with our grievance officer, email jaykumar2470f@gmail.com. We respond within the timelines required by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Permissions we ask for

ORBII requests location, microphone (for the voice trigger only), notifications, and photo access (for your profile picture). You can revoke any permission in your phone's settings, though some safety features will stop working without them.

Security & retention

We protect your information with encryption in transit and access controls, and we keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or as required by law. Incident records are retained so you have your own history; you can delete them by deleting your account.

Children

ORBII is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has used ORBII can contact us to have the data removed.

Changes & contact

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above. For any privacy question, reach us at jaykumar2470f@gmail.com.

Emergency reminder: ORBII is a safety tool, not a replacement for the police, ambulance, or fire services. If your life is in immediate danger, call 112.
