Good questions

Answers before you ask.

Does Voice SOS record me all the time?

No. Listening happens entirely on your phone to recognise a distress word, and the audio is never uploaded or stored anywhere. Nothing leaves the device.

What if I trigger it by accident?

Every SOS starts a 10-second countdown you can cancel with one tap, so a false alarm never reaches anyone.

Does it work when the app is closed?

Yes. Voice SOS keeps listening in the background so you're protected without opening the app first.

Is it really free?

Yes. The core safety features — Voice SOS, circle alerts, nearby responders, and one-tap 112 — are free for every woman. Plus adds the verified responder network.

Do you sell my location?

Never. No data brokers, no ad profiling. It's the whole reason ORBII exists.

Is ORBII a replacement for the police?

No. ORBII helps you reach help faster, but in immediate danger you should always call 112. We make that one tap away.
