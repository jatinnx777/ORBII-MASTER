# ORBII Campus Ambassador Pack

Everything you need to start. Read it once, keep it, ask if anything is unclear.

## What ORBII is

ORBII is a women's safety app built in India. The thing people notice first is
**hands-free Voice SOS**: you say a word and your phone raises an alarm without
you touching it. It listens on your phone, so your voice never leaves the device.

When it fires, your trusted circle gets your live location, nearby ORBII users
are alerted, and 112 stays one tap away.

**Why you matter.** A safety network is worth what its density is worth. One
woman with ORBII on a campus of eight thousand is protected by her circle. Two
hundred women with ORBII on that campus are protected by each other. You are not
selling downloads, you are building the thing that makes it work.

## Your first week

Do these four. Nothing else is required.

**1. Set it up on your own phone first.** Fire a test SOS. Add someone to your
circle. You cannot explain something you have not used, and people will ask you
questions in the first five minutes.

**2. Run one session.** Fifteen minutes in a hostel common room, a class, or a
society meeting. Do not present. Ask two or three people to install it and try
Voice SOS while everyone watches. The demo does the work; you just have to be
in the room.

**3. Make one group.** A WhatsApp group where people can ask you things. Pin your
code in it.

**4. Tell us one thing that is broken.** This is the part we care about most and
most programmes never ask for. If Voice SOS does not trigger in your hostel
corridor, we want to know this week, not in a survey at the end of term.

**Time it takes:** about three to five hours a week.

## Your code

Your code looks like `SRMS01`. It is on your poster and it should be pinned in
your group.

**Where people type it.** When somebody sets up ORBII, on the first screen where
they enter their name, there is a field near the bottom:

> Campus ambassador code (optional)

They type your code there. That is how you get credit.

**If they already installed it without the code**, they cannot add it later. Tell
people to enter it while they are setting up, not after.

**Your dashboard is at orbii.in/ambassador.** Sign in with the same email you use
in the app. It sends you an eight digit code, there is no password.

## What to say, and what not to

**People will ask you hard questions. Answer them honestly.** Getting caught
overselling costs you the campus. Saying "that part is not finished yet" costs
you nothing and makes everything else you say believable.

### What works today

Voice SOS. Trusted circles. Safe zones. Safe Journey. Evidence recording.
One-tap 112. Location history that deletes itself every night at midnight.

### What is not finished

**The offline Bluetooth mesh and the SMS lifeline are built but still being
tested on real phones.** They are not live. Say exactly that.

**The Community Helper Network is live across Delhi NCR only.** It is expanding
city by city. If your campus is outside that, the core protections still work
everywhere in India, but there is no responder network near you yet. Say that
too.

### Questions you will get

**"Is it free?"**
Yes. Voice SOS, circles, safe zones and 112 are free. There is a paid tier for
verified responders and some extras.

**"Is it listening to me all the time?"**
It listens on your phone only, and only when you turn Voice SOS on. Your audio
is never uploaded. It never leaves the device.

**"Who can see my location?"**
Only the people you put in your circle, and only during an emergency or when you
choose to share. It is deleted every night at midnight.

**"Do you sell my data?"**
No. Not to colleges, not to advertisers, not to anybody. That is not a policy we
can change later, it is the reason the app exists.

**"Is this only for women?"**
Anybody can use it. It is designed around what women in India actually deal with,
which is a different thing from being restricted.
