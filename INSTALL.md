# Installing ORBII (Android)

> Share this with anyone who's trying to install ORBII from the APK link.

ORBII isn't on the Google Play Store **yet**, so when you install it directly,
Android shows a few safety warnings. **This is normal for every new app** — it
doesn't mean ORBII is unsafe. The app is signed by ORBII and your safety data
stays yours. Here's how to get past the warnings (takes 30 seconds).

---

## Step 1 — Download the app
Tap the ORBII download link. Your browser may say
**"This type of file can harm your device."**
Tap **Download anyway** / **OK**.

## Step 2 — Open the file
Open the downloaded file (`orbii.apk`) from your notifications or the
**Downloads** folder. Tap it.

## Step 3 — Allow installs (first time only)
If Android says **"For your security, your phone can't install unknown apps
from this source"**, tap **Settings → Allow from this source** (turn the
toggle ON), then go back.

## Step 4 — Get past Play Protect
You may see **"Blocked by Play Protect"** or **"This app may be harmful."**

- Tap **More details** (or the small arrow / "More info").
- Tap **Install anyway** (sometimes "Install without scanning").

## Step 5 — Install
Tap **Install** → wait a few seconds → **Open**. Done. 🎉

---

## If it still won't install
- **"App not installed"** → you may already have an older ORBII installed with
  a different signature. **Uninstall the old ORBII first**, then install again.
- Make sure you have ~200 MB free storage.
- Some phones (Xiaomi/MIUI, Oppo, Vivo) hide the "Install anyway" button —
  briefly turning OFF *Play Protect scanning* in the **Play Store → profile →
  Play Protect → Settings** lets it through, then turn it back on.

---

### Why does this happen?
Google Play Protect distrusts any app installed outside the Play Store. The
**permanent fix is publishing ORBII on the Play Store**, which removes all of
these warnings — that's on our roadmap. Until then, these steps are safe to
follow.
