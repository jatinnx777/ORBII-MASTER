# ORBII — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 June 2026
**Last updated:** 4 August 2026

ORBII ("ORBII", "we", "us", "our") is a personal-safety application that helps
you get help quickly in an emergency by alerting people you trust and people
near you. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and your
choices. We've tried to keep it plain.

**Contact:** orbiisafety@gmail.com
**Data Protection / Grievance Officer (DPDP Act, 2023):** privacy@orbii.in

We act as a **Data Fiduciary** under India's Digital Personal Data Protection
Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"). Our lawful basis for processing is your **consent**,
which we ask for clearly during sign-up and which you can withdraw at any time.

---

## 1. The short version
- We collect only what's needed to keep you safe: your account details, your
  emergency contacts, and your location (used when the app is open / during an
  SOS).
- **Your voice and any SOS audio are processed and stored only on your device.
  They are never uploaded to our servers or shared.**
- During an SOS, your name and live location are shown to the people you chose
  (your circle / emergency contacts) and to nearby ORBII users who can help.
- We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

---

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information**
- Name, phone number, profile photo, and username (whichever you provide when
  signing up). If you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google account
  info (name, email, profile photo).

**Emergency contacts**
- The name, phone number, and relationship of the contacts you add, so we can
  alert them in an emergency.

**Location**
- Precise location (GPS), used **while the app is in use and during an SOS** to
  show your position to your circle and nearby helpers and to find help near
  you. ORBII does **not** track your location in the background. We do not
  request background-location permission.

**Audio (on-device only)**
- With your permission, ORBII listens for your chosen safety phrase (e.g. "help
  help") using on-device speech recognition, and may record a short audio clip
  when you fire an SOS. **This audio is processed and stored entirely on your
  device. It is never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.** You can
  play back or delete SOS recordings from your device.

**Push notification token**
- A device token (via Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can notify
  you when someone in your circle needs help.

**Diagnostics**
- Basic crash and error logs (what failed, and a short trail of recent in-app
  actions) so we can fix problems and keep the safety features reliable.

**Payments**
- If you buy a subscription, payment is processed by **Razorpay**. We do not
  receive or store your card / UPI details; we only receive a confirmation that
  a payment succeeded.

We do **not** collect your contacts list, browsing history, or device files.

---

## 3. How we use your information
- To send an SOS: alert your circle and emergency contacts with your name and
  live location (by in-app notification, push, and/or SMS).
- To show you nearby SOS alerts so you can help others.
- To detect your safety phrase (on-device) and trigger an SOS hands-free.
- To run the app: sign-in, your circle, your settings, subscriptions.
- To keep it reliable and safe: diagnostics and abuse-prevention.

---

## 4. Who can see your information

**Other ORBII users (only when you choose / during an SOS):**
- The people you add to your **circle** and your **emergency contacts** can be
  alerted and shown your name and live location during an SOS.
- When you fire an SOS, **nearby ORBII users** may see the alert with your
  approximate/live location so they can come help. This is the core purpose of
  the app.

**Service providers (processors acting on our behalf):**
- **Supabase** — secure database + authentication hosting.
- **Expo / Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** — delivering push notifications.
- **Razorpay** — processing payments.
- Your mobile carrier — if an emergency SMS is sent to your contacts.

**Colleges and institutions:** where a college or organisation pays for ORBII
licences for its students or members, that only means those people *have* ORBII.
The institution gets **no dashboard, no student locations, no history, and no
data access of any kind**. Your location is visible only to the circle you
choose. We do not build control-room or admin data feeds, and we never hand your
data to an institution.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it with
advertisers.

**Where your data is stored:** our database and authentication are hosted by
Supabase. We do not transfer your personal data to any country or territory
restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

---

## 5. SMS to your emergency contacts
When you fire an SOS, ORBII can send your emergency contacts an SMS containing
your name and a live-location link, so they're reached even with no internet or
if they don't use ORBII. SMS is sent only as part of an SOS you initiate.
Standard carrier charges may apply.

---

## 6. How long we keep it
- Account info, emergency contacts, and circles: until you delete them or your
  account.
- SOS records (location/time): retained as your safety history; active alerts
  are limited to a short window for nearby users.
- **Live-location history (circle breadcrumbs): automatically deleted after 48
  hours.** We do not keep a long-term trail of where you have been.
- On-device audio: stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall.
- Diagnostics: kept for a limited period to fix issues.
- When you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account & data), your
  personal data is erased without undue delay.

---

## 7. Security
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider.
- Access is restricted with row-level security so users can only read their own
  data and the limited, nearby SOS information needed to help.
- Auth tokens are stored in your device's secure keystore.
No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

---

## 8. Your rights under the DPDP Act
As a Data Principal you have the right to:
- **Access** a summary of the personal data we process about you.
- **Correction / completion / update** of your data — edit your profile and
  contacts directly in the app.
- **Erasure:** delete your account and all associated data yourself in the app
  (**Settings → Delete my account & data**), or by emailing
  **privacy@orbii.in**. This erases your profile, circles, contacts, and
  location history.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (turn off location sharing, revoke
  permissions, or delete your account). Withdrawing does not affect processing
  already carried out.
- **Grievance redressal:** contact our Grievance Officer at **privacy@orbii.in**.
  We aim to respond within the timelines set by the DPDP Act.
- **Nominate** another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death
  or incapacity — email us to register a nominee.
- **Permissions:** you control location, microphone, notifications, and SMS
  permissions in your device settings; the app still works (with reduced
  features) if you decline.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

---

## 9. Age and children
ORBII is intended for users **aged 18 and above**. You must confirm you are 18
or older when you create an account. We do not knowingly let a person under 18
create an account on their own. Where the law permits a minor to use ORBII (for
example, for their safety), we require **verifiable consent of a parent or
lawful guardian** before processing the minor's data, and we do not track or
profile children or serve them advertising.

---

## 10. Important safety disclaimer
ORBII is a technology platform that facilitates emergency communication and
safety assistance. ORBII does not guarantee emergency response, rescue,
intervention, prevention of harm, or the availability of any third party,
including police, emergency responders, or nearby helpers. Always contact
official emergency services (112 in India) in a real emergency.
